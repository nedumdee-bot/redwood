Questions

Good things to know

Do we meet online or in person?

Sessions are held online by video or phone, which keeps things flexible and comfortable. Just let me know your preference when you book.

What if I’m not sure what I need?

That’s completely normal — and exactly what the free discovery call is for. We’ll talk it through and find the right starting point together.

Is everything confidential?

Yes. Your privacy matters. What we discuss stays between us, within professional and legal limits.

Is this the same as therapy or medical care?

Counseling and mentorship offer support, guidance, and practical tools. They are not a substitute for emergency services or medical or psychiatric treatment. If you’re in crisis, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line.
