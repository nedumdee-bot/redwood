Questions

Good things to know

Do you work with businesses like mine?

Very likely! I work with owners across retail, trades, wellness, hospitality and professional services — from solo founders to small teams. If you’re unsure, ask me on the free call.

Do we meet in person or online?

Both. Most sessions happen over video call, which keeps things flexible, but in-person is possible locally. Just let me know your preference when you book.

What if I’m not sure which service I need?

That’s completely normal — and exactly why the discovery call is free. We’ll talk it through and I’ll recommend the option that fits your goals and budget.

Is there a long contract?

No lock-ins. Monthly coaching is rolling and you can pause or stop whenever you like.
