My story

Fifteen years, hundreds of conversations

I spent the first part of my career inside growing companies — in operations, then finance — watching brilliant ideas stall for want of a simple plan. When I started advising owners directly, I found my calling.

Today I work with founders and small teams who want a trusted guide: someone who’ll ask the right questions, help them see clearly, and stick with them through the messy middle. No jargon, no lectures — just honest, practical help.

When I’m not with clients, you’ll find me in the garden, walking the dog, or badgering my family with spreadsheets they didn’t ask for.
