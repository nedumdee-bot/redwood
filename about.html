My approach

A safe, judgment-free space

My approach is client-centered, goal-oriented, and grounded in active listening and real-world strategies. With experience in both private and group mentorship settings, I create a space where clients feel genuinely supported and encouraged.

Together, we explore what’s weighing on you, make sense of it, and build practical tools you can carry into everyday life. There’s no rush and no judgment — just steady, thoughtful support at a pace that feels right for you.

Areas I support

  • Stress, anxiety, and life transitions
  • Personal growth and self-confidence
  • Career direction and decision-making
  • Communication skills and emotional regulation
  • Building healthier habits and mindset
